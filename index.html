Each square is one day. The strip along its bottom is coloured by the town you're in, and on travel days it splits left to right at about the hour you move. Dots count the scheduled items on that day. A faded day number means nothing is booked that day, one of the open stretches of the trip. The raised square is the day the page is showing, and today's day number is amber. Click any day to move the whole page to it.
